Licenses of the atlas files in this folder
==========================================

These files come with the extension so that the Neuro workflows run without a download. They are not covered by the
extension's own license (MIT, see LICENSE at the top of the repository): each keeps the license of its source, given
below. The files are unchanged copies of the originals (the NIfTI files only gzip compressed), except where noted.


1. MNI ICBM 152 2009c Nonlinear Symmetric atlas
-----------------------------------------------

Files:
    mni_icbm152_t1_tal_nlin_sym_09c.nii.gz         T1 template
    mni_icbm152_t1_tal_nlin_sym_09c_mask.nii.gz    brain mask
    mni_icbm152_gm_tal_nlin_sym_09c.nii.gz         grey matter probability map
    mni_icbm152_wm_tal_nlin_sym_09c.nii.gz         white matter probability map

Source: https://nist.mni.mcgill.ca/icbm-152-nonlinear-atlases-2009/

2. CerebrA
----------

Files:
    mni_icbm152_CerebrA_tal_nlin_sym_09c.nii.gz    CerebrA labels
    CerebrA_LabelDetails.csv                       the names of the labels

Source: https://nist.mni.mcgill.ca/cerebra/ ; dataset DOI: 10.12751/g-node.be5e62

License: Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Public Domain Dedication (as stated on the dataset's DOI page,
https://doi.gin.g-node.org/10.12751/g-node.be5e62/ ; https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

Please cite:
    AL Manera, M Dadar, V Fonov, DL Collins. CerebrA, registration and manual label correction of Mindboggle-101
    atlas for MNI-ICBM152 template. Scientific Data 2020;7:237.


3. CerebrA with pons (made for this extension)
----------------------------------------------

Files:
    mni_icbm152_CerebrA_tal_nlin_sym_09c_wpons.seg.nrrd
    mni_icbm152_CerebrA_tal_nlin_sym_09c_wpons.csv

The CerebrA labels (2) with the pons added as a structure of its own, inside the brainstem, by the extension's
author. A derivative of CerebrA; the addition is given under the same terms (CC0 1.0). Cite CerebrA (2).

CerebrA_groups.csv (the broad regions of the grouped atlases) is part of the extension (MIT).
